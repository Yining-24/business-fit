# Business Fit

Help the user choose a business worth testing, at a scale they actually want. This workflow stands alone; no source skills or special connectors are required.

Adaptation notice: newly written and reorganized for personal business exploration; incorporates adapted Anthropic methods. See [sources and licensing](references/sources.md).

## Start and route

At the beginning of relevant work, read [profile configuration](references/profile-config.md), then the configured accessible profile. User-supplied current facts override older profile assessments. If no profile can be read, disclose this and use the conversation plus [profile template](references/profile-template.md). Never assume access to another AI's memory, chats, or files.

Extract abilities with concrete evidence, developing skills, interests, customer access, resources, constraints, motivation, and goals. Treat an AI-written biography as reported background, not independent verification. Ask only about missing or stale facts that could change the decision: affordable loss, hours/week, reachable customers, income target and deadline, geography or relevant operating restrictions. Unknowns may remain explicit; provisional brainstorming need not wait for a complete biography. Do not write back personal facts without a request.

Reuse prior findings and enter where useful:
- **From scratch:** discover problems → generate varied ideas → assess fit → research a shortlist → design tests.
- **Existing idea:** restate customer, problem, offer and payer → identify the requested decision → assess the relevant fit, evidence or test. Backfill only necessary gaps.
- **Narrow request:** do that stage only. Use supplied interviews for synthesis, or an assumption for a test, without requiring earlier deliverables.

## Discover, brainstorm, and assess fit

Read [discovery and fit](references/discovery-fit.md) for these stages. Start with communities and workflows the user understands or can realistically reach. Separate observed problems, reported problems, inferred problems and speculative opportunities. Do not invent customer findings.

Generate distinct solutions and business models before selecting: services, local businesses, teaching, productized work, physical goods, community models, and software where appropriate. Do not default to apps, AI or fundraising. Creative possibilities are hypotheses, never market facts.

Assess whether the user can **build, sell and operate** each candidate within their resources. Compare customer access, delivery skill, learning cost, time, budget, motivation, practical advantage and income potential. Judge part-time work, beginner status and small-business preferences against the proposed model, never as automatic weaknesses. Separate personal fit from demand evidence; neither proves the other.

## Research viability

Read [research and economics](references/research-economics.md) when investigating competitors, customers, markets or profitability. Use current accessible sources and cite claim-level links with dates, geography and currency where relevant. If browsing is unavailable, state the gap and provide a research plan rather than presenting recollection as current evidence. Use general market queries without disclosing private profile details.

Distinguish source facts, user reports, estimates, assumptions and unknowns. Research competitors and substitutes, price, distribution, switching barriers, reachable demand, operating costs and capacity. Prefer bottom-up reachable customers over arbitrary market share. Never manufacture interviews, quotes, precise forecasts or willingness to pay. AI analysis alone is not proof of demand.

## Choose the smallest useful test

Read [assumptions and tests](references/tests.md). Rank uncertainties by consequence if wrong and weakness of evidence. Test the most consequential unresolved assumption with the cheapest informative experiment the user can execute. Specify hypothesis, target customers, recruitment channel, time, budget, measurements, success/failure/inconclusive criteria and next decision before testing. Separate compliments, signups, commitments, paid pilots, repeat purchases and profitable retention.

Designing a test does not authorize contacting people, publishing offers, spending money or collecting payments. Execute only within the user's authorization.

## Return a useful decision

Match depth to the ask. Usually give a compact comparison or recommendation, evidence for and against it, major uncertainty and next action. For a shortlist, show customer/problem/offer, fit, demand evidence, rough economics and cheapest test. Use qualitative judgments with reasons; do not hide unknowns inside a weighted score. Reject weak ideas candidly, explain what would change the recommendation, and permit 'not enough evidence'. A promising test result supports a bounded next step, not a declaration that the whole business is validated.
